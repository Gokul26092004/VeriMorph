NATIONAL DISASTER MANAGEMENT COMMISSION EMERGENCY BULLETION
Bulletin Number: NDMA-CYC-2026-04
Date & Time of Issue: 28-09-2026, 18:00 IST
Subject: Pre-Landfall Alert - Super Cyclonic Storm 'VARUN' Over Central Bay of Bengal

Executive Summary:
The Super Cyclonic Storm 'VARUN' has intensified rapidly over the West-Central Bay of Bengal and is moving north-northwestwards at a speed of 18 km/h. As per radar telemetry and satellite observations, landfall is projected along the northern coastal belt between Puri and Sagar Island during the early morning hours of 30-09-2026.

Key Meteorological Observations:
- Maximum sustained surface wind speed: 175-185 km/h gusting to 205 km/h.
- Estimated central atmospheric pressure: 938 hPa.
- Projected storm surge: 4.5 to 6.0 meters above astronomical tide, likely to inundate low-lying coastal tracts of Kendrapara, Balasore, and South 24 Parganas.

Threat Impact & Potential Damage:
- Total disruption of power transmission networks, telecommunication towers, and optical fiber links across coastal districts.
- Extensive damage to thatched houses, uprooting of large banyan and coconut trees, and severe inundation of national highways NH-16 and state roads.
- Major danger to standing kharif crops, marine fishing vessels, and coastal aquaculture infrastructure.

Emergency Directives & Evacuation Measures:
1. Total suspension of all marine fishing operations across northern and central Bay of Bengal; fishermen out at sea instructed to return to harbour immediately.
2. Mandatory mass evacuation of approximately 450,000 residents living within 5 km of the high-tide line to designated cyclone shelters by 29-09-2026, 14:00 IST.
3. Pre-positioning of 36 National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) battalions equipped with satellite phones, high-capacity tree cutters, inflatable rescue boats, and emergency medical trauma units.
4. Activation of round-the-clock district emergency operations centers (DEOCs) and public emergency broadcast via cell broadcast alert systems.
